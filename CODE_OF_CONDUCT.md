# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical facts and reproducible evidence.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Any behavior that creates a hostile environment.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting
Please report unacceptable behavior through private channels defined in SUPPORT.md.
